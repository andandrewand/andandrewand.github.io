Summary

I'm a systems designer helping organisations get technology working inside and out.

I find where value gets stuck between people, process and technology, then design the system that frees it up. The tool changes, whether it's a programme, a prototype, a workflow or an AI agent, but the job doesn't: get the right people connected and working better. Technology is the facilitator.

My background spans enterprise and government consulting at IBM iX, service design and product management at Singapore Global Network (part of Singapore's Economic Development Board), and creative agency work across Southeast Asia. I hold an MA in Service Design from the Royal College of Art.

Open to

  • AI experience and service design: designing human + agent workflows, conversational experiences, and AI-enabled services.
  • AI product management: taking AI products from discovery and prototype through pilot and adoption.
  • AI deployment and forward deployed strategist roles: working with enterprise customers to scope, deploy, and drive adoption of AI.

Based in Singapore. Happy to work across APAC. English and conversational Mandarin.
